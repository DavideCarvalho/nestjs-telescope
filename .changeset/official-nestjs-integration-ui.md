Cards and nav entries for the entries `@dudousxd/nestjs-telescope-official` records from NestJS's first-party channels: `resilience` (Resilience), `workflow` (Nest workflows) and `auth` (Auth). Each card shows the event as a badge (red for a refusal or failure), its subject (the policy and entrypoint, the workflow and step, or the user / the denied `Policy.ability`), every scalar field the entry carries, the error, and the workflow output or status. The entries table labels them by event and subject, and labels `mail` entries `[status] subject`.
